ModeMania

A gallery of electromagnetic modes. Each panel is the field profile of a mode, computed from a numerical 2D eigenmode solve, so the fundamental, first, and second-order shapes are the real ones for each structure, not sketches.

Guided modes

A dielectric ridge (refractive index n = 4, on a perfect-conductor floor). The colour map is the out-of-plane electric field Ez. The mode is pulled up in order from a single central lobe to transverse and vertical nodal patterns.

−+Ez

Magnetic polarization

The same dielectric ridge, now capped by a thin perfect-conductor layer (10% of its height). The field is the out-of-plane magnetic field Hz (TE polarization), which meets a Neumann condition at every metal surface. Shown are the cavity modes trapped under the cap.

−+Hz

Surface plasmon polariton

A metal grating: teeth on a metal substrate. Corrugating a conductor traps the field in the grooves and binds it to the surface, a spoof surface plasmon. At the far-infrared wavelengths where a real metal such as gold has a very large permittivity it behaves almost as a perfect conductor, so this ideal-conductor model is the right picture there. The colour map is the in-plane field Ey built from the solved Hz.

−+Ey
