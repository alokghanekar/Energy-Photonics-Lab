ModeMania

Interactive visualizations of electromagnetic modes. Each card solves the fields live in your browser, so you can see how a structure's modes change with its geometry and materials.

Guided modes

A dielectric ridge (index n) on a perfect electric conductor. The colour map is the out-of-plane field Ez of the selected mode, found from a live 2D eigenmode solve. Change the width, height, index, and mode order and watch the pattern, ordering, and resonant wavelength respond.
