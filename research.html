Research

We study how nanostructure, material response, and symmetry can be used to control thermal radiation. Our work combines electromagnetic and thermal modeling, nanophotonic design, and experiments to connect fundamental light–matter interactions with energy and thermal technologies.

Broken-symmetry metasurfaces

Breaking mirror, rotational, or translational symmetry to produce directional, polarization-selective, and chiral thermal emission.

Selected work

  • Electro-optic symmetry breaking of BIC modes for tunable infrared emissivity. Materials Today Physics, 2023.
  • Reversible symmetry breaking of BIC graphene plasmons for tunable mid-infrared absorption. Optics Express, 2024.
  • Active tuning of angular asymmetric thermal emission via carrier injection in metal-insulator-metal resonance gratings. Surfaces and Interfaces, 2024.

Kirchhoff's law violation

Space-time modulation and other time-varying media that break reciprocity, enabling nonreciprocal thermal emission that violates Kirchhoff's law.

Selected work

  • Violation of Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation with a space-time modulated grating. ACS Photonics, 2022.
  • Nonreciprocal thermal emission using spatiotemporal modulation of graphene. ACS Photonics, 2022.

Thermophotovoltaics

Wavelength-selective emitters and absorbers matched to a photovoltaic cell for efficient conversion of heat to electricity.

Selected work

  • Novel and efficient Mie-metamaterial thermal emitter for thermophotovoltaic systems. Optics Express, 2016.
  • Mie-metamaterials-based thermal emitter for near-field thermophotovoltaic systems. Materials, 2017.
  • Optimal design of a wavelength-selective thermal emitter for thermophotovoltaic applications. J. Thermal Science and Engineering Applications, 2018.

Thermal management and intelligent energy systems

Heat-transfer modeling and control for building energy, HVAC, and battery thermal management, from thermal diodes to reduced-order models.

Selected work

  • High-contrast far-field radiative thermal diode. Scientific Reports, 2017.
  • High-rectification near-field thermal diode using a phase-change periodic nanostructure. Applied Physics Letters, 2016.
